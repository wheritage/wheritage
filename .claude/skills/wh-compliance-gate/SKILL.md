# Compliance gate

Sources: `.agents/product-marketing.md` §6–§8, `business-os/GARDE-FOUS.md`. Run BEFORE drafting (to
spot traps) and AFTER (to list flags). Never claim content is "AMF-safe".

Check, and list each hit under **Compliance flags**:

1. **Figures.** Only the 2026 RRSP limit $33,810 is pre-verified (re-check canada.ca at filming). Every
   other number is "illustrative example", needs the accountant's OK, and must never read as a client result.
2. **Cash incentives** (e.g. "$50 if you sign"): cabinet approval required first.
3. **Banned claims:** "$0 tax", guaranteed coverage/returns, "cheapest", universal exclusions, named
   product recommendations, unapproved testimonials, disparaging comparisons.
4. **Title / firm name:** only the confirmed wording « Conseiller en sécurité financière · Industrielle Alliance »; check the firm's required disclosure wording for ads and emails.
5. **Disclaimer:** public content and lead magnets carry « Information générale seulement, pas un conseil
   personnalisé. » Emails keep a `[DIVULGATION]` line.
6. **Privacy (Loi 25 / CASL):** no client data in examples; consent before marketing email; blank/sample
   policies only.
7. **Brand separation:** W Heritage and Leo never share a format, product or CTA. Leo = auto insurance
   only, keyword `MAGASINE`, never `DM SOUMISSION`.
8. **Language:** no FR/EN mixing in one video.

If nothing is found, write "Compliance flags: None found" — never leave the section out.
