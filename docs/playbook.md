## 1. The format (conversational)

| Part | Rule |
|---|---|
| Hook | Fixed, exact wording. A readable claim or a specific number. Never a polite question. Taken word for word from the hook bank. |
| 3 ideas | Loose talking points. Said in Walid's own words, like a chat with someone he has known for years. No memorized script. |
| CTA | Fixed: `Écris + keyword` (EN video: `Comment + keyword`). One keyword per reel, from the DM list. |
| Length | 30-40 s in the final cut, from about 60 s of raw. |
| Takes | 2-3 takes. Keep the most natural one, not the cleanest one. |
| Language | One video = one language. FR (Quebec, tutoiement) and EN (Ontario) are two separate native recordings. |

## 2. The 8-video batch method

The sprint films 2 reels per session, Tue and Thu, 9:00-11:00 Casablanca (UTC+0). A batch of 8 videos is 4 sessions, or two weeks of filming. This gives the editing week its raw material.

### Prep (the day before, or Tue/Thu at 8:30)

1. **Hooks printed exactly.** Print or display each hook word for word, taken from the hook bank. Do not retype from memory.
2. **One reel card per video** (section 1). Three ideas per card, a few words each. No sentences to recite.
3. **Whiteboard frame 1 per reel.** Write the claim with the number visible (for example "10 000 $/mois" as the house example) on the board before the camera rolls. Frame 1 is the first thing the viewer reads. Photograph each board once as a backup.
4. **Props (suggestion):** whiteboard and markers that read at phone distance, phone tripod, a clean wall or window light, a lav mic if one exists, a printed hook sheet at eye level beside the lens, a charger and a phone with free storage.
5. **Figures check.** Every number on a board is either the verified RRSP limit ($33,810, re-check canada.ca at filming) or an illustrative example the accountant has OK'd. Do not film a number that has not cleared this. See section 5.
6. **Language check.** Decide FR or EN for each reel before the first take. Do not switch mid-video.

### Filming session (9:00-11:00 Casablanca)

Two reels in two hours. **(suggestion) timing:**

| Time | Step |
|---|---|
| 9:00-9:15 | Set up, frame, check sound, board 1 up |
| 9:15-10:00 | Reel A, 2-3 takes of about 60 s |
| 10:00-10:10 | Reset, board 2 up |
| 10:10-10:50 | Reel B, 2-3 takes of about 60 s |
| 10:50-11:00 | Back up, name files, note the best take |

Delivery on camera: one take, talk to the lens like a friend. Say the hook exactly, then the 3 ideas loosely, then the CTA exactly. Leave a beat of silence before and after each idea so the jump cuts are clean **(suggestion)**. Phone settings **(suggestion)**: vertical 9:16, rear camera, 1080p, 30 fps, lock exposure and focus, airplane mode on.

Mark the best take as you go **(suggestion)**: say the take number aloud, or note it on the reel card.

### File naming (suggestion)

`YYYYMMDD_reel-slug_KEYWORD_LANG_take1.mp4`

Example: `20261201_buy-sell-1_CONVENTION_FR_take2.mp4`

Keep the slug the same on the board photo, the raw takes, the project and the export so a file is found in seconds.

### Backup (suggestion)

Copy the day's clips off the phone the same day to the computer, then to a second place (external drive or cloud). Do not clear the phone until both copies exist. Keep raw takes until the reel has been posted and the numbers reviewed.

## 3. CapCut Pro recipe

**Target: 25-35 minutes per video.** Night-editing workflow: edit on the Tue/Thu night after filming, so the reels are ready for the next posting slots. Menu names change between CapCut versions, so this recipe says "in CapCut" instead of naming exact menus.

### Steps (suggested time per step)

1. **Import and pick the take (3 min).** Import the best take. Keep the most natural one. The raw is about 60 s.
2. **Rough cut to 30-40 s (8 min).** Cut out dead air, repeats and filler. Jump cut between the 3 ideas. One idea is one block. Keep Walid's natural voice and pace.
3. **Frame 1 (3 min).** The first frame must show the readable claim with the number visible. Use the whiteboard shot, or add the claim as a text overlay. Do not open on a logo, a greeting or a title card.
4. **Captions (5 min).** Use CapCut's auto captions, then read every word and fix mistakes, especially numbers, accents (é, è, ç) and French terms. Captions stay on screen the whole video.
5. **Visual change every 2-3 s (6 min).** See the retention table below.
6. **Audio (3 min).** Voice first. Any sound effect or music sits under the voice, never over it, and sound effects vary from beat to beat.
7. **Cover, export, file (3 min).** See sections 3 and 4.

Total: about 31 min. Stay inside 25-35 by not polishing the speech. Lighter editing is the point of the format.

### Caption style (from `brand.md` and `design.md`)

- Cream text (`#F7F2EA`) on dark, gold (`#C9A84C`) for the key number or word only.
- Serif for titles (Cormorant Garamond), Montserrat for body text. If those fonts are not in CapCut, use the closest available and do not add decoration. Gold confirmed by Walid as "a warm but premium gold": use `#C9A84C`. `[TO CONFIRM: final type pairing]`
- No rounded corners on caption boxes. No glow, no gradient text, no glass effect, no emoji, no em dashes in on-screen copy.
- Keep captions inside the 9:16 safe area, above the bottom Instagram UI zone.
- Text reveals: lines rising in, not letter-by-letter typewriter **(from the CLAUDE.md house rules)**. Where CapCut offers a text animation, pick a simple rise or fade.
- Word-by-word captions are listed in the product-marketing research as a retention tactic. Use them if they stay calm and readable. Check this against "never TikTok-aggressive". `[TO CONFIRM: Walid's preference]`

### Retention rules

- **A visual change every 2-3 seconds.** The change can be a *visual* (a cut, a zoom, an overlay, a b-roll shot, a board change), a *sound* (a sound effect or music beat), or *movement* (a keyframed push-in, a text rising in).
- **One of each per video** (visual, sound, movement). **Rotate the combinations across the week** so Wed, Fri, Sat and Sun do not feel the same. **(suggestion)** A simple rotation: Wed leans on visuals, Fri on sound, Sat on movement, Sun on a mix. Walid can change it.
- **Open loop held to the end.** Frame 1 gives the claim. Idea 3 holds the answer or "the mix" until the last seconds, just before the CTA. Do not give the answer in idea 1.
- **Pace.** The house rules say a slow, confident rhythm: no 0.5 s jump cuts, no shakes, no emoji pops. A change every 2-3 s is a steady change, not a flash. Use eased, gentle zooms. No bounce or overshoot. If a keyframed zoom in CapCut feels bouncy, use the smoothest ease available.
- **Retention is not the goal alone.** The audience is a niche (owners of $300k+ businesses). Success is watching to the end, saving, and DMing. It is not millions of views.

### CapCut features (only the ones that exist)

Auto captions, cutting and splitting clips, keyframes (for a slow push-in), text and text templates, audio (voice, music, sound effects, volume), and export. Anything beyond these: do it "in CapCut" in whatever way the current version offers. Do not trust a menu path in this file.

### Export for 9:16 Reels

- 9:16 vertical, 1080 x 1920, 30 fps, MP4 **(suggestion on resolution and frame rate: match the filming settings)**.
- Resolution and quality: choose the highest option CapCut offers within what the footage supports.
- Check the audio before exporting: voice clear, no sound effect louder than the voice.
- Watch the export once, full screen, with sound, then once muted. Both must work.
- Name the exported file with the same pattern as the raw files, ending `_final.mp4` **(suggestion)**.

### DM keyword automation reply (suggestion)

The comment-keyword to DM automation is the best-converting mechanism among peers (9+ creators use it). Set the automated reply to keep the same keyword as the reel's CTA.

```

Information générale seulement, pas un conseil personnalisé.
```

- One keyword = one reply. The reply must match what the CTA promised.
- Do not offer a cash incentive in the reply.
- The reply is French for an FR reel and English for an EN reel.
- Consent and CASL: do not add the person to marketing email from a DM alone. `[TO CONFIRM: tool used for the automation and its consent wording]`

### Cover frame (suggestion)

- Use the whiteboard frame 1: one claim, one number, one face or object. Gold number on black, cream text.
- Keep the text readable at thumbnail size and inside the safe area for the profile grid crop.
- Any number that is not the $33,810 RRSP limit carries the wording "exemple illustratif" (EN: "illustrative example").
- Same look across all 16 reels so the grid reads as one series.

## 5. Compliance gate before posting

Run this for every reel, every time. It is a flag list. It does not certify anything as compliant, and nothing here may be called "AMF-safe". Sources: `wh-compliance-gate` skill, `product-marketing.md` §6-§7, `business-os/GARDE-FOUS.md`.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] **Figures.** Each number is either (a) the verified 2026 RRSP limit of **$33,810** (re-check canada.ca at filming), or (b) an **illustrative example** that the accountant has OK'd. Illustrative numbers are never presented as a client result and carry "exemple illustratif" on screen. No invented figures.
- [ ] **Cash incentives.** None in the video, caption or DM reply (for example "$50 if you sign"), unless the cabinet has approved it first.
- [ ] **Banned claims.** No "pay $0 tax", no guaranteed coverage or returns, no "cheapest", no universal exclusions, no named product recommendations, no unapproved testimonials, no disparaging comparisons.
- [ ] **Title and firm name.** The confirmed lower-third title is **"Conseiller en sécurité financière · Industrielle Alliance"**. Walid confirmed it for video (2026-11-24 brief). It is **still to be checked against the firm's required disclosure wording** before it goes on a posted reel. Until that check is done, flag it on every reel that shows it. `product-marketing.md` §1, `brand.md` and `design.md` now carry the same confirmed wording.
- [ ] **Disclaimer.** The caption carries "Information générale seulement, pas un conseil personnalisé."
- [ ] **Privacy (Loi 25 / CASL).** No client data in examples. Blank or sample policies only. Consent before any marketing email.
- [ ] **Brand separation.** W Heritage and Leo never share a format, product or CTA. Leo is auto insurance only (keyword `MAGASINE`). Never mix them in one video, caption or DM flow.
- [ ] **Language.** One language per video, caption and DM reply.
- [ ] **Mapping.** The reel maps to one of the 7 pillars and one DM keyword.
- [ ] **Hook.** The hook matches the hook bank word for word. A new hook is labelled "NEW, for Walid's approval".

**Every reel card ends with a "Compliance flags" section.** List each figure (verified or illustrative), any incentive, any guarantee/"cheapest"/"$0 tax" wording, and any title or firm name. If there are none, write "None found". Never leave the section out.

If something is uncertain, flag it to Walid. Do not smooth it over.
